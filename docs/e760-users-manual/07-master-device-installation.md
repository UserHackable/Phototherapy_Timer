7. MASTER DEVICE INSTALLATION
The MASTER device (or simply the “MASTER”) is the device with the timer and switchlock. It is
always the first device needed in an E-Series system. If your device is a "CLINICAL MASTER"
(indicated by a "C" after the 3 digits in the model#), see instead the "E-Series E740-HEX FULL
BOOTH WITH BASEPLATE Assembly Instructions User's Manual Supplement", and the "Solarc C01
Timer User's Manual Supplement", if so equipped.

To prevent devices from falling, it is very important that one device in the system be fastened to the
wall at the top, with the bottom of the device and all its weight resting on the floor. This is typically the
MASTER device. It is NOT acceptable to “hang” devices on the wall. Devices are always installed
vertically - never mount devices horizontally so the patient lies down for treatment.

SPECIAL CASE: Alternatively, for full booths only, which is typically six (6) devices arranged in a
hexagon, "Locking Struts" are available to lock the devices in position so together they form a fixed
stable assembly, in which case none of the devices need to be fastened to a wall. See section
"Larger Systems & Full Booths".


           DANGER: One (1) device must be fastened to the wall as directed.
           Do NOT “hang” devices on the wall - all the weight must rest on the floor.
           Never mount devices horizontally so the patient lies down for treatment
           Failure to may result in device(s) falling causing severe injury.


The MASTER can be mounted two different ways: 1) against a flat wall, or 2) in a corner.
If ADD-ON devices might be added later to expand your system, mounting the MASTER against a flat
wall is necessary so there is space on the sides for more devices. This is the preferred mounting.
If the MASTER will only ever be used by itself, it can alternatively be corner mounted, but beware that
dropping something behind it will require that the device be dismounted for retrieval.

The following MASTER device installation procedure is done with the bulbs in place, so take great
care during your work to avoid breakage. First time installation should take 10 to 20 minutes.
Tools required: Phillips (cross+) screwdriver, pencil (assuming walls are of "drywall" board).

Carefully unpack the MASTER and lay it face up flat on the floor. If any damage has occurred, stop this
assembly procedure and contact Solarc Systems toll free 866-813-3357 (705-739-8279).

1. For mounting against a flat wall: Move the MASTER device to
   the mounting location and stand it up vertically so it leans against
   the wall, with the bulbs facing the wall. Attached to the back side
   of the top endcap are two silver upper mounting brackets.
   Rotate the two brackets 90 degrees upward so they protrude
   above the top as shown. Spin the device around so the bulbs
   face outward and the mounting plates face the wall. At the
   desired mounting location, with the bottom of the device pushed
   up tightly against the baseboard, position the device as
   perfectly vertical as possible, and mark the mounting bracket
   hole locations on the wall with a pencil. If the baseboard is very
   thick, if ADD-ON devices might be added, and/or if the floor is
   not flat; it may be necessary to install spacers (not supplied)
   between the mounting brackets and the wall so the MASTER
   stands more vertical, to improve how the devices move with
   each other. This can be done later if necessary.
   ALTERNATIVELY, for devices with four or more bulbs, instead
   of using the upper mounting brackets, two upper through-
   holes are available between the bulbs as shown here ►.
2. For corner mounting: Move the MASTER to its final mounting
   location with the bottom of the unit pushed up against the
   baseboards and mark the position of the diamond ♦ shaped
   holes with a pencil.
3. Place the device aside and, with a Phillips (cross+) screwdriver,
   screw the supplied white plastic threaded wall inserts fully into
   the drywall at the pencil marks. Since all the weight of the
   device is resting on the floor, it is not necessary that the
   mounting plate screws be fastened to a wall stud.
4. Locate the device into position and fasten the brackets to the
   wall using the screws provided. Corner mounted devices
   require screws at least 4 inches long (not included).

5. If ADD-ON devices will be connected, use the screws provided
   to fasten the two MASTER device lower mounting brackets to
   the baseboard as shown here ►. These brackets prevent the
   bottom of the MASTER device from being pulled away from the
   wall when the devices are moved. ALTERNATIVELY, for
   devices with four or more bulbs, and if your baseboard is at
   least 3 inches high, you can use the two through-holes between
   the bulbs as shown in the lower picture.
6. Attach the power supply cord to the power inlet on the top
   endcap and route the cord to the electrical receptacle.
7. The hardware kit for each Master device includes three plastic
   push-in CORD-CLIPS for use in the 1/4" hole on the top endcap
   of the device and two 1/4" holes along the back side of the
   device. These clips can be used to retain the power supply
   cord for clean, organized routing to the electrical outlet.
8. IMPORTANT: This equipment requires use of an adequately
   sized three prong grounded power supply cord as described in
   SPECIFICATIONS TABLE 2. Never modify the power cord
   so that it will fit in a two prong outlet. If an extension cord is
   required, ensure that it is: (a) in good condition, (b) 3-prong
   grounded type, (c) same or heavier wire gauge as the supplied
   power supply cord, and (d) as short as practical. Do not run
   cords under carpeting. Arrange cords to minimize tripping
   hazard. If there are concerns regarding power quality in your
   area, consider use of a surge protector.

            WARNING: Improper use of the power supply cord and grounding plug can result in
            risk of electrical shock. Never modify the power cord so that it will fit in a two-prong
            outlet.

9. The timer is located in the front top center of the MASTER, behind the “ATTENTION” paper label.
    Read the notes and remove the label to expose the timer functions. The notes can then be discarded.
10. The timer was fully tested when the MASTER was built; however, to ensure that there was no
    shipping damage, it is suggested that the timer be retested by comparing it to a digital watch. Turn the
    switchlock ON, set the timer and the watch each for 2 minutes, put on the goggles supplied, and start
    the timer and watch simultaneously. Leave the room immediately with the watch and check from
    behind the door that the bulbs turn off and agree with the watch. If the timer is suspect in any way, do
    not operate the system and contact Solarc Systems. Assuming a successful test, the system is now
    ready for operation.
11. Note that the bulbs in all new devices are “burned-in” at Solarc Systems. There is no need to perform
    the “burn-in” procedure outlined in the “Bulb Replacement” section of this manual.
12. If you might later need to re-ship the device(s), consider saving the packing materials. If not, note that
    the packing foam is made from polyethylene (PE) and may be recyclable.
