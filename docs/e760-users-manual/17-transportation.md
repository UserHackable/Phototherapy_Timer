17.    TRANSPORTATION
For transportation, the bulbs should be left in place, the switchlock key removed, and the system
disassembled into either: (A) single devices for the lowest weight per lift (but that leaves the bulbs
exposed), or (B) for maximum protection, pairs of devices of the same frame size, folded up face-to-
face to each other, and fastened together at all four corners at the hinge lugs.
