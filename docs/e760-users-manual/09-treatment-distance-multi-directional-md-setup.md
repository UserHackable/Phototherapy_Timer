9. TREATMENT DISTANCE & MULTI-DIRECTIONAL (MD) SETUP

             WARNING: The minimum recommended exposure distance is 8 to 12 inches (20-
             30cm) from the front of the bulbs to the nearest skin surface of the body. Standing
             too close to the bulbs increases the amount of ultraviolet light delivered to the skin
             and may cause skin burns.

When two or more devices are connected, the hinge points and
bottom sliders allow the devices to be easily positioned so
ideally all the bulbs are at the recommended treatment
distance of 8 to 12 inches from the nearest skin surface.
The objective is to conform the devices to the shape of the
patient, by forming a C-shape. A patient with a larger mid-
section will therefore have a more “open” C-shape system.

To perform a multidirectional setup, center the patient in front of
the devices, and using a ruler or tape measure, use the handles
to move the devices so the nearest skin surfaces are, if
possible, all 8 to 12 inches (20-30cm) from the bulbs. In front of
each device on the floor, put a piece of tape or make a mark so
the same device locations can be verified before each treatment. Similarly, mark the locations of the
patient’s feet to ensure the correct treatment distance. The lock-nuts on the hinge lugs can be fully
tightened to help hold the device locations.
