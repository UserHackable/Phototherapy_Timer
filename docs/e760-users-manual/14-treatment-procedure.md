14.      TREATMENT PROCEDURE
The following procedure is for Solarc's standard timer. If your MASTER device is for use in a
phototherapy clinic, it may be equipped with the "C01" timer firmware, in which case see the "Solarc
C01 Timer User's Manual Supplement".


           WARNINGS – TREATMENT PROCEDURE
 •    Ultraviolet radiation. Follow instructions. Avoid overexposure. As with natural sunlight,
      overexposure can cause eye and skin injury, and allergic reactions. Overexposure can cause
      severe burning. Repeated exposure may cause premature aging of the skin and skin cancer.
      Wear protective eyewear; failure to may result in severe burns or long-term injury to the eyes.
      Medications, however taken, and cosmetics may increase sensitivity to ultraviolet radiation.
      Consult a physician before using ultraviolet lamps. Consult a physician if you are using
      medications or have a history of skin problems or believe yourself especially sensitive to sunlight.
 •    The instructions from your physician shall always take priority over this User’s Manual.
 •    Use of this system must be accompanied by physician examination (skin check) at least once per
      year; this is a condition of sale. Consult your physician if you have not had promising results after
      2 to 4 months of diligent treatments.

1. Review your treatment log and prepare for treatment according to the results of your previous
    treatment and your specific Exposure Guideline Table.
2. Cover skin areas that should have minimal UV exposure, such as the genitals (unless affected)
    and skin areas that have received considerable natural sun exposure in your lifetime; such as the
    face, head, shoulders, forearms and upper chest. These areas can be covered using a mask or
    clothing such as a thong. Women should consider wearing a bra to prevent overexposure. For
    men, a cut-off sock can be used to cover both the penis and the scrotum.
3. Advise others in the household that you are about to use the ultraviolet light and that they are not
    to enter the room. Parents/guardians should diligently supervise persons in their care.
4. Make sure the devices are in the correct position by checking their alignment with the location
    marks you made on the floor.
5. Turn the switchlock to the ON position. The timer will display the last treatment time setting.
6. Set the timer to your treatment time for your first body position, by pressing the INCREASE (Up
    Arrow▲) to increase the setting, or the DECREASE (Down Arrow▼) to decrease.
7. Double check that the timer is set to the correct treatment time. For example, do not make a
    “factor of ten” error, such as entering 3:00 when you really want 0:30.
8. Cover your eyes with the ultraviolet protective goggles supplied. All persons exposed to the
    ultraviolet light must also wear ultraviolet protective goggles.
9. Take your first body position and press the timer START/STOP button to begin the treatment.
10. Immediately check that the timer is counting down properly. If you suspect any malfunction, leave
    the room immediately, stop all treatments and contact Solarc Systems.
11. Press START/STOP at any time to turn off the lights. Press START/STOP to resume.
12. After the time is complete, the lights will turn off, you will hear three (3) “triple-beeps”, and the
    display will reset to your previous time setting.
13. If you need to treat another body position, change the time setting as required, reposition your
    body, and press the timer START/STOP button to begin the treatment.
14. Immediately check that the timer is counting down properly.
15. Repeat for each body position. Be careful not to overlap areas.
16. Turn the switchlock to the OFF position. Remove & hide the key to prevent unauthorized use.
17. Remove the goggles and store them with the system so they are easy to find next time.
18. Record your treatment times and body positions in your treatment log or calendar.
19. After about 24 hours, record the result of your treatment.
