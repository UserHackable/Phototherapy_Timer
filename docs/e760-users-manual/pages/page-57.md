** If your skin ever becomes more than slightly pink at any stage in the schedule, drop back to the previous exposure
time. If you become uncomfortably red or sunburned after treatment, skip the next one or two exposures and drop back
to the previous exposure time. If more than one treatment is missed in succession, reduce the exposure time further.
When in doubt, always use the lower exposure time. Never get burned!
