16.    VITILIGO TREATMENT DISCUSSION
For vitiligo, the optimal dose is when the depigmented white skin areas (the “lesions”) reach a state of
very mild erythema, which is the slightest onset of sunburn. For example, 6 to 24 hours after a
treatment, the lesions should ideally reach a very light pink colour, but it should NOT be solid bright
pink or red. Depending on the individual, as the bulbs age and lose power, and as your skin builds
tolerance; this could mean that your treatment times have to be increased beyond the maximum
listed in your Exposure Guideline Table, but if so typically only after many months of use. Whatever
the case, always remember, NEVER GET BURNED!

For significant vitiligo involvement, Solarc recommends full-body phototherapy with both healthy and
vitiligo-affected skin areas receiving the same dose, so there are both local effects (in the lesion) and
systemic effects (from the healthy skin, such as Vitamin D and other UVB induced biologic
processes).

When taking UVB-Narrowband treatments, vitiligo patients should avoid natural sunlight, because the
UVA light it contains may cause additional pigmentation of the healthy skin and greater contrast to
the white vitiligo lesions.

17.    TRANSPORTATION
For transportation, the bulbs should be left in place, the switchlock key removed, and the system
disassembled into either: (A) single devices for the lowest weight per lift (but that leaves the bulbs
exposed), or (B) for maximum protection, pairs of devices of the same frame size, folded up face-to-
face to each other, and fastened together at all four corners at the hinge lugs.

18.    MAINTENANCE
We recommend cleaning the bulbs, reflectors (and Clear Acrylic Window if so equipped) once every
year or two, or as needed. The dirt that builds up on the surfaces inhibits transmission of the light to
your body and results in longer treatment times.

To clean a device:
   1. Wear both safety glasses and gloves when working near the bulbs.
   2. Detach the power supply cord and remove the key from the switchlock.
   3. Remove the wire guard or Clear Acrylic Window. To remove the wire guard, remove the
       screws holding the black plastic P-clips on both sides of the guard, pull up on the guard to
       release it from behind the lower endcap, and then push down on the guard to release it from
       behind the upper endcap.
   4. For a basic cleaning, keep the bulbs in place, spray some window cleaner such as Windex©
       onto the bulbs, reflector and CAW; and wipe all around. Alternatively, to clean the CAW wash
       it with soap and warm water in a bathtub. For a more complete cleaning job, remove the bulbs
       per the instructions in the Bulb Replacement section that follows.
   5. If necessary, vacuum any dirt from the channels around the bottom of the bulbs.
   6. Re-install the wire guard or Clear Acrylic Window. To install the wire guard, push up on the
       guard so the vertical wires at the top insert behind the top endcap flange, then push down on
       the guard so the vertical wires at the bottom insert behind the lower endcap flange. Then
       reinstall the black plastic P-clips. Note that the guard for the MASTER has one less horizontal
       wire than ADD-ON guards, for clearance at the timer.
   7. Reattach the power supply cord.
   8. If the device was very dirty, reduce treatment times.
