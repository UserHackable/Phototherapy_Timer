Some Bulbs Do Not Turn On

First, before proceeding below, if one or more entire devices do not turn on, check that the device
connection cables between the devices are properly connected as described in the ADD-ON DEVICE
INSTALLATION section.

The bulbs are electrically grouped in pairs to a "ballast". For 2-bulb "E720" devices, there is only one
ballast. For 4-bulb "E740" devices there are two ballasts, with the two outermost bulbs wired to one
ballast, and the two innermost bulbs wired to the other ballast. For 6-bulb "E760" devices, a third
ballast is wired to the “middle bulbs” (in bulb positions 2 and 5 counting left to right); and so on. The
bulbs are always electrically grouped in pairs in a symmetrical pattern to the vertical centerline of the
device.

For a group of two bulbs wired to the same ballast to operate properly, both bulbs must be functional.
That is, one bad bulb or lampholder connection will cause the other bulb to also not function. So, if a
group of two bulbs wired to the same ballast does not turn on, there are two likely possibilities:
a) one of the two bulbs is defective, or not installed correctly in the sockets,
b) the ballast is defective.

To determine which, if available, swap two known working bulbs with the two non-working bulbs and
retest. Refer to the Bulb Replacement section of this manual for instructions on bulb removal as
necessary. If the problem follows the bulbs, then they are the problem source and one at a time put a
non-working bulb back in to pinpoint which bulb has failed. Check the condition of the end pins and
the sockets and retest. If the problem stays with the ballast, then it is defective and will need
replacement.

If known working bulbs are not available (for example if you have only a single 2-bulb MASTER
device), the best option may be to speculate and change the ballast, because a ballast is less
expensive than bulbs and is easier to ship. Contact Solarc for ballast replacement parts and
procedures.

Timer Failure

If a timer fails or behaves erratically, please contact Solarc for further instructions and do not use the
system.
