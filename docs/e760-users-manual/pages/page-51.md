APPENDIX D
EXPOSURE GUIDELINE TABLES "EGTs"                               (TREATMENT TIMES)
This appendix includes the following UVB-Narrowband Exposure Guideline Tables (EGTs). These
important tables will guide you through your initial and ongoing treatment times. For each EGT group
there is a specific page for psoriasis, vitiligo and eczema.

Do the following:
1. Carefully select the EGT group for the SYSTEM you have, be it a single panel or one of the
    multidirectional (MD) arrangements.
2. Select the skin condition to be treated, and in the case of psoriasis your Skin Type.
3. To avoid mix-ups, with a pencil cross off any tables not used.
4. Consider moving or copying your specific table to the front of this User's Manual for quick
    reference.

    Take great care to select the correct table! Please contact Solarc for help if necessary.

Multidirectional (MD) systems, and in particular those consisting of three or more devices, are
effective for the simultaneous treatment of the front (or back) of the body and a portion of both sides,
so that typically only two treatment positions are required (front & back), and to reduce the patient's
total treatment time. The devices should ideally be angled so that they are all 8 to 12 inches from the
bulbs to the closest skin surface.

A multidirectional (MD) system provides its greatest irradiance (light power) in the lateral center of the
device assembly, with somewhat less irradiance moving outward towards the ADD-ON devices. To
equalize the amount of light to all skin areas, patients may consider rotating their body during
treatments. Alternatively, if some skin areas need more light than others, consider targeting those
areas on the MASTER device.

The treatment times listed are based on the system's maximum nominal steady state Irradiance as
listed in SPECIFICATIONS TABLE 2, located at the front of this User's Manual.
