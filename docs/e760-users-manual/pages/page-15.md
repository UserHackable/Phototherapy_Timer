   to fasten the two MASTER device lower mounting brackets to
   the baseboard as shown here ►. These brackets prevent the
   bottom of the MASTER device from being pulled away from the

6. Attach the power supply cord to the power inlet on the top
   endcap and route the cord to the electrical receptacle.
7. The hardware kit for each Master device includes three plastic
   push-in CORD-CLIPS for use in the 1/4" hole on the top endcap
   of the device and two 1/4" holes along the back side of the
   device. These clips can be used to retain the power supply
   cord for clean, organized routing to the electrical outlet.
8. IMPORTANT: This equipment requires use of an adequately
   sized three prong grounded power supply cord as described in
   SPECIFICATIONS TABLE 2. Never modify the power cord
   so that it will fit in a two prong outlet. If an extension cord is
   required, ensure that it is: (a) in good condition, (b) 3-prong
   grounded type, (c) same or heavier wire gauge as the supplied
   power supply cord, and (d) as short as practical. Do not run
   cords under carpeting. Arrange cords to minimize tripping
   hazard. If there are concerns regarding power quality in your
   area, consider use of a surge protector.

            WARNING: Improper use of the power supply cord and grounding plug can result in
            risk of electrical shock. Never modify the power cord so that it will fit in a two-prong
            outlet.

9. The timer is located in the front top center of the MASTER, behind the “ATTENTION” paper label.
    Read the notes and remove the label to expose the timer functions. The notes can then be discarded.
10. The timer was fully tested when the MASTER was built; however, to ensure that there was no
    shipping damage, it is suggested that the timer be retested by comparing it to a digital watch. Turn the
    switchlock ON, set the timer and the watch each for 2 minutes, put on the goggles supplied, and start
    the timer and watch simultaneously. Leave the room immediately with the watch and check from
    behind the door that the bulbs turn off and agree with the watch. If the timer is suspect in any way, do
    not operate the system and contact Solarc Systems. Assuming a successful test, the system is now
    ready for operation.
11. Note that the bulbs in all new devices are “burned-in” at Solarc Systems. There is no need to perform
    the “burn-in” procedure outlined in the “Bulb Replacement” section of this manual.
12. If you might later need to re-ship the device(s), consider saving the packing materials. If not, note that
    the packing foam is made from polyethylene (PE) and may be recyclable.
