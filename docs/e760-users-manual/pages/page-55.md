* If the time since your last treatment has been more than 3 days, take the following action:
4-7 days: Keep the same dose                             2 or more weeks: Start over
1-2 weeks: Decrease dose by 50% or start over

** If your skin ever becomes more than slightly pink at any stage in the schedule, drop back to the
previous exposure time. If you become uncomfortably red or sunburned after treatment, skip the next one
or two exposures and drop back to the previous exposure time. If more than one treatment is missed in
succession, reduce the exposure time further. When in doubt, always use the lower exposure time.
Never get burned!

Consult your Physician regarding the best strategy for long term maintenance.
