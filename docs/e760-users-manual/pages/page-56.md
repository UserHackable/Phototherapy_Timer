MD262          6-bulb MASTER with a 2-bulb ADD-ON on each side
               Multidirectional with 10 bulbs total

An E-Series "MD262-UVBNB" is a "Multidirectional" (MD) UVB-Narrowband phototherapy system
consisting of a 6-bulb MASTER device with a 2-bulb ADD-ON device attached on each side, for a
total of ten (10) bulbs; as shown here ► .

The MD262 is effective for the simultaneous treatment of the front (or back) of the body and a portion
of both sides, so that typically only two treatment positions are required (front & back), and to reduce
the patient's total treatment time. The devices should be angled so that they are all 8 to 12 inches
from the bulbs to the closest skin surface.

The MD262 provides its greatest irradiance (light power) in the lateral center of the MASTER device,
with somewhat less irradiance moving outward towards the ADD-ON devices. To equalize the
amount of light to all skin areas, patients may consider rotating their body during treatments.
Alternatively, if some skin areas need more light than others, consider targeting those areas on the
MASTER device.
