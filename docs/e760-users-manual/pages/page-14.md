The following MASTER device installation procedure is done with the bulbs in place, so take great
care during your work to avoid breakage. First time installation should take 10 to 20 minutes.
Tools required: Phillips (cross+) screwdriver, pencil (assuming walls are of "drywall" board).

Carefully unpack the MASTER and lay it face up flat on the floor. If any damage has occurred, stop this
assembly procedure and contact Solarc Systems toll free 866-813-3357 (705-739-8279).

1. For mounting against a flat wall: Move the MASTER device to
   the mounting location and stand it up vertically so it leans against
   the wall, with the bulbs facing the wall. Attached to the back side
   of the top endcap are two silver upper mounting brackets.
   Rotate the two brackets 90 degrees upward so they protrude
   above the top as shown. Spin the device around so the bulbs
   face outward and the mounting plates face the wall. At the
   desired mounting location, with the bottom of the device pushed
   up tightly against the baseboard, position the device as
   perfectly vertical as possible, and mark the mounting bracket
   hole locations on the wall with a pencil. If the baseboard is very
   thick, if ADD-ON devices might be added, and/or if the floor is
   not flat; it may be necessary to install spacers (not supplied)
   between the mounting brackets and the wall so the MASTER
   stands more vertical, to improve how the devices move with
   each other. This can be done later if necessary.
   ALTERNATIVELY, for devices with four or more bulbs, instead
   of using the upper mounting brackets, two upper through-
   holes are available between the bulbs as shown here ►.
2. For corner mounting: Move the MASTER to its final mounting
   location with the bottom of the unit pushed up against the
   baseboards and mark the position of the diamond ♦ shaped
   holes with a pencil.
3. Place the device aside and, with a Phillips (cross+) screwdriver,
   screw the supplied white plastic threaded wall inserts fully into
   the drywall at the pencil marks. Since all the weight of the
   device is resting on the floor, it is not necessary that the
   mounting plate screws be fastened to a wall stud.
4. Locate the device into position and fasten the brackets to the
   wall using the screws provided. Corner mounted devices
   require screws at least 4 inches long (not included).
