** If your vitiligo lesions ever become more than slightly pink at any stage in the schedule, drop back to the
previous exposure time. If you become uncomfortably red or sunburned after treatment, skip the next one or
two treatments and drop back to the previous exposure time. If more than one treatment is missed in
succession, reduce the exposure time further. When in doubt, always use the lower exposure time.
*** Vitiligo Treatment Time Discussion:
For vitiligo, the optimal dose is when the depigmented white skin area (the “lesion”) reaches a state of
very mild erythema, which is the slightest onset of sunburn. For example, 6 to 24 hours after a treatment,
the lesion should ideally reach a very light pink colour, but it should NOT be solid bright pink or red.
Depending on the individual, as the bulbs age and lose power, and as your skin builds tolerance; this could
mean that your treatment times have to be increased beyond the maximum listed above, but if so typically
only after many months of use. Whatever the case, always remember, NEVER GET BURNED!
For significant vitiligo involvement, Solarc recommends full-body phototherapy with both healthy and
vitiligo-affected skin areas receiving the same dose, so there are both local effects (in the lesion) and
systemic effects (from the healthy skin, such as Vitamin D and other UVB induced biologic processes).
Consult your physician regarding the best strategy for long term maintenance and maximum
treatment time / dose limits.
