13.     EXPOSURE GUIDELINES

               WARNINGS – EXPOSURE GUIDELINES


 •    Ultraviolet radiation. Follow instructions. Avoid overexposure. As with natural sunlight,
      overexposure can cause eye and skin injury, and allergic reactions. Overexposure can
      cause severe burning. Repeated exposure may cause premature aging of the skin and skin
      cancer. Wear protective eyewear; failure to may result in severe burns or long-term injury
      to the eyes. Medications, however taken, and cosmetics may increase sensitivity to
      ultraviolet radiation. Consult a physician before using ultraviolet lamps. Consult a
      physician if you are using medications or have a history of skin problems or believe
      yourself especially sensitive to sunlight.
 •    The instructions from your physician shall always take priority over this User’s Manual.
 •    Use of this system must be accompanied by physician examination at least once per year;
      this is a condition of sale. Consult your physician if you have not had promising results
      after 2 to 4 months of diligent treatments.


The primary objective with UVB phototherapy is to establish the safest effective dosage.
The objective is to get ONLY enough ultraviolet light to control your skin disorder and to
minimize your total lifetime cumulative ultraviolet dose and its potentially harmful side effects.

In many cases, the purchase of a home UVB system is preceded by UVB phototherapy at a clinic or
hospital. With regular visits, the dosage will be fairly well established based on the frequency of visits,
the number and condition of the bulbs, device efficiency and other factors. However, because there is
no way of quantifying your previous treatment dosage, switching to a home UVB system requires that
the dosage level be re-established from the beginning.
