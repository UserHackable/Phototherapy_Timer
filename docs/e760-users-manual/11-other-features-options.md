11.    OTHER FEATURES & OPTIONS

Patient Fan (-PF Option, for devices with wire guards only)

For ADD-ON devices equipped with wire guards, an optional built-in fan is available to help keep the
patient cool during treatment. Air is directed onto the patient from along the vertical center band of the
device and is controlled by an ON-OFF switch at the top. The Patient Fan is operational only when
the bulbs are on. The Patient Fan option must be specified at the time of ordering the device, for
example by ordering model# E740A-UVBNB-PF.

Clear Acrylic Window (-CAW Option)

A Clear Acrylic Window (CAW) is a thin sheet of UVB-transmissive plastic that covers and protects
the bulbs, for use instead of wire guards. The CAW:
• Is highly recommended, especially for a full booth used in a phototherapy clinic.
• Provides superior protection of the bulbs from patient contact and breakage,
• Prevents the patient from touching the hot surfaces of the bulbs,
• Reduces dust and dirt build-up around the bulbs, reflector and bottom lampholders,
• Includes a fan that evacuates heat from the bulb enclosure, which helps keep the patient cool,
• Does not significantly change the device irradiance,
• Makes it easier and safer to exit from a full booth, because the patient can push anywhere on the
    doors to open them,
• Is not compatible with the Patient Fan (-PF) option,
• Is available on all E-Series devices except E720 2-bulb "Mark1" devices,
• Must be specified at the time of ordering, for example by ordering model# E740M-UVBNB-CAW,
• Should be specified for all the devices in a system,
• Is cleaned in-place using a spray-on cleaner such as Windex© and a soft cloth. Alternatively, if
    the CAW is very dirty, it should be removed and cleaned with soap, warm water, and a soft cloth;
    in a bathtub perhaps.

Towel Hooks

Towel Hooks are located on the two rear upper corners of every
device. Just bend these sheet metal tabs out by hand to create a
place to hang your towel or other articles of clothing. Alternatively, the
holes in the center of the tabs could be used to string a cord to hang a
curtain in front or behind the device(s).

Gap Seals

When two devices are connected together, a 1/4 to 1/2 inch wide gap is
created between the sides of the devices. “Gap-Seals” are thin flexible film
strips that cover over that gap to: A) minimize the chance that fingers get
pinched in the gap, and B) reduce the amount of ultraviolet light that leaks
out from the gap into the room.

For HOME users, Gap-Seals are typically NOT necessary because
treatments are usually in private. They are, however, available on request,
and at no charge.

For a FULL BOOTH in a CLINIC, Gap-Seals may or may not be installed
depending on clinic preferences. For more information see the "E-Series
E740-HEX FULL BOOTH WITH BASEPLATE Assembly Instructions User's
Manual Supplement".
