16.    VITILIGO TREATMENT DISCUSSION
For vitiligo, the optimal dose is when the depigmented white skin areas (the “lesions”) reach a state of
very mild erythema, which is the slightest onset of sunburn. For example, 6 to 24 hours after a
treatment, the lesions should ideally reach a very light pink colour, but it should NOT be solid bright
pink or red. Depending on the individual, as the bulbs age and lose power, and as your skin builds
tolerance; this could mean that your treatment times have to be increased beyond the maximum
listed in your Exposure Guideline Table, but if so typically only after many months of use. Whatever
the case, always remember, NEVER GET BURNED!

For significant vitiligo involvement, Solarc recommends full-body phototherapy with both healthy and
vitiligo-affected skin areas receiving the same dose, so there are both local effects (in the lesion) and
systemic effects (from the healthy skin, such as Vitamin D and other UVB induced biologic
processes).

When taking UVB-Narrowband treatments, vitiligo patients should avoid natural sunlight, because the
UVA light it contains may cause additional pigmentation of the healthy skin and greater contrast to
the white vitiligo lesions.
